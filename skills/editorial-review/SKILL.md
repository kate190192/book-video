# Skill: Independent Editorial Review

Act as an independent editor, not the original writer. Review the script against the evidence ledger and topic brief. Do not assume the draft is correct.

Check:
1. Core question and thesis clarity.
2. Argument validity, causal inference, and missing premises.
3. Fact/source accuracy and author-claim vs consensus distinction.
4. Counterarguments, uncertainty and scope limits.
5. Narrative progression, opening payoff, conclusion closure.
6. Redundancy, empty transitions, cliché, overly fragmented prose and chapter-by-chapter summary.
7. Spoken-language clarity and pacing.
8. Copyright, privacy, attribution and disclosure risks.

Output `06_editorial/review_report.md` with score by rubric dimension, line/section-specific issues, severity, proposed fix, hard-stop result, and PASS/REVISE/FAIL. Do not rewrite silently; report substantive changes. PASS requires >=85 and no hard-stop issue. Maximum two revision cycles, then block for human review.
