# Skill: Title, Thumbnail and Metadata

Create at least three title options and two thumbnail concepts. Each title must accurately name the book or central question and make the specific value clear without unsupported claims. Score clarity, specificity, curiosity, accuracy and audience fit; do not use fabricated quotations or fake controversy.

Thumbnail concepts must specify:
- primary visual/metaphor;
- short readable text (prefer 2–6 Chinese words);
- visual hierarchy and mobile legibility;
- contrast and focal point;
- asset provenance and license;
- how it complements rather than duplicates the title.

Description must include a concise content summary, book bibliographic details verified from sources, key references, chapter timestamps only when measured, and relevant AI/copyright/affiliate disclosures if applicable. Do not add irrelevant trending tags.

Outputs:
- `09_metadata/title_options.md`
- `09_metadata/thumbnail_brief.md`
- `09_metadata/video_description.md`
- `09_metadata/metadata.json`

The human operator may choose among candidates; if no explicit selection policy exists, present the recommended option with rationale rather than pretending an image was created.
